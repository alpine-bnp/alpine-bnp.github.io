## about-introduction

Three days of scientific exchange, hiking and time together in the mountains.

## about-paragraph-one

Alpine BNP is a small scientific meeting devoted to Bayesian nonparametrics and related areas, held immediately after the Bayesian Nonparametrics conference in Bergamo.

## about-paragraph-two

The informal format combines invited and contributed talks, poster sessions, shared meals and time outdoors, with space for conversations beyond the scientific sessions.

## programme-note

Provisional programme. Exact timings, speakers and talk titles will be announced closer to the meeting. Outdoor activities are weather-dependent.

## getting-there

Participants will meet in Bergamo on Saturday morning. Organised shuttles will take the group to the trailhead, followed by a group hike to the rifugio. Departure time, meeting point and transport arrangements will be announced later.

## hiking-emphasis

Reaching the venue requires hiking.

## hiking-paragraph

Participants must be physically able to make the approach on foot. Distance, elevation gain, hiking time, difficulty and recommended equipment will be confirmed once the route is finalised.

## accommodation

Participants will spend two nights in shared rooms at the rifugio or associated hostel, with shared bathrooms and showers. Final room arrangements will be communicated closer to the event.

## monday-routes

Monday offers a standard descent along the approach route or an optional longer mountain itinerary via a second rifugio. The longer route is intended for participants comfortable with a longer Alpine hike; the final route and difficulty remain to be confirmed.

## weather-note

Mountain weather can change rapidly. Outdoor plans may be adapted for safety.

## registration-introduction

Participation will initially be offered to invited participants. A limited number of additional places will then be made available through an open call, subject to accommodation and meeting capacity.

## registration-details

The final fee, inclusions, application and registration deadlines, payment instructions and cancellation policy will be announced before registration opens.
